# Reusable training infrastructure

## What it was

Training kits and resource hubs that helped distributed teams run programs on their own.

## Why it mattered

Teams needed a way to deliver training without depending on one central team.

## What success looked like

FY26: 13 training kits.

## How

- Created training kits and resource hubs.
- Made the materials reusable by distributed teams.
- Enabled teams to run programs independently.
