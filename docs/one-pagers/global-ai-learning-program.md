# A global AI learning program

## What it was

A global program that helped people learn about AI through events.

## Why it mattered

The program brought AI learning to a wide audience, including people who were new to it.

## What success looked like

FY26: 121 events reached 36,900 attendees; 71% of users were new to the program.

## How

- Delivered AI learning through events.
- Reached learners around the world.
- Welcomed users who were new to the program.
